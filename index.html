THE ARCHITECTURE

How TITANNOVA Works

The ecosystem is organized into a 14-spot matrix at each active level. Placement, spillover, payouts and recycle actions are executed by the smart contract according to its programmed rules.

01

Registration

A participant registers through the smart contract using the configured token payment and receives the Registration Pass NFT, subject to the 100,000-pass limit.

02

Matrix Placement

Each level uses 14 spots. The contract determines placement and eligible receivers according to the matrix rules.

03

Upgrade

Levels 1–11 can activate the next level through the programmed cycle-1 Spot 4 and Spot 5 mechanism.

04

Recycle

When a 14-spot cycle completes, the matrix recycles according to the contract. Spot 4 is allocated to the NFT Pool and Spot 5 to the Royalty Pool.

NFT ECOSYSTEM

Registration Pass & Level-6 NFT

100,000 Registration Passes

The Registration Pass supply is capped at 100,000. Once the cap is reached, the contract can issue the configured Level-6 NFT to eligible Level-6 achievers according to its rules.

Transferable, Not Burned

The Level-6 NFT can be transferred. NFT ownership and Rank achievement are separate: transferring the NFT does not transfer the Rank or its royalty eligibility.

ROYALTY SYSTEM

4 Ranks • 25% Each

The configured royalty system uses four ranks. Each rank receives 25% of the distributable royalty amount for the 30-day period, subject to the deployed contract's eligibility and accounting rules.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Is TITANNOVA centralized?

The intended design is to execute core matrix and fund-routing rules through the smart contract without discretionary admin fund withdrawals. The deployed bytecode and verified source code should be used to confirm the final permissions.

Does NFT transfer transfer Rank?

No. Under the stated specification, NFT ownership can transfer, but Rank achievement and royalty eligibility remain associated with the address that achieved the Rank.

What happens to Spot 4 and Spot 5?

For the stated final plan, Level 12 and recycle use Spot 4 for the NFT Pool and Spot 5 for the Royalty Pool. Cycle-1 Levels 1–11 use the Spot 4/5 upgrade mechanism.

Where can I verify activity?

Use the deployed contract's official BNB Smart Chain explorer page and the contract's verified source code.
