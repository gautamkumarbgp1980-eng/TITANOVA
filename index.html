Frequently Asked Questions

What is TITANOVA?

TITANOVA is a decentralized matrix ecosystem interface designed around a 12-level structure and a 14-spot Cycle 1.

What is the Level 1 entry amount?

The defined Level 1 amount is 0.001 BTCB. Each next level follows a 2× progression.

How many spots are in Cycle 1?

Cycle 1 contains 14 spots. Spot 14 completes the cycle through the defined Recycle step.

Is income guaranteed?

No. The website does not guarantee income, profit or financial returns. Blockchain and digital-asset activities involve risks.

How should I verify the contract?

Verify the final contract address, source code, network, transaction amount and transaction history before signing.

Will NFT and rank transfer together?

This depends on the final deployed contract logic. NFT ownership and rank state should be checked separately against the verified contract.
